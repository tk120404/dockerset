Features

Everything runs locally on your Mac. Dockerset lives in the menu bar and never needs an account.

🗂Saved layouts

Keep separate sets of apps, folders, links, spacers and widgets for work, home or anything else. Capture your current macOS Dock in one click.

🖥Native, custom, or both

Rewrite the macOS Dock when you switch layouts, replace it with the Dockerset Dock, or run both side by side.

🌙Focus switching

Pair a layout with a macOS Focus through a Shortcuts automation that opens dockerset://layout/Work.

🎨Appearance

Icon size, position, Liquid Glass and other materials, opacity, corner radius, magnification and auto-hide. Icons shrink to fit, like the macOS Dock.

📁Folders that look like Finder

Folder stacks, the folder's initial on its icon, and Finder tag colours.

↔Scroll area

Send any icons into a compact strip with ‹ › arrows to keep a long Dock tidy.

🪟Windows, like the Dock

Right-click an app for its window list, New Window, and Show All. Minimized windows appear in the Dock and restore in a click.

💾Backup & move

Export layouts and settings (optionally with your macOS Dock) and restore them later or on another Mac.

🛟Safe by design

Your original macOS Dock is saved before the first change, and a watchdog brings it back if Dockerset ever quits unexpectedly.

Widgets

Live information right in the Dock. Click any widget for details.

🎵Now Playing

Track, artist, artwork and controls for Spotify and Apple Music.

📅Calendar & Reminders

Your next event with a Join button for Zoom, Meet and Teams links, plus reminders you can tick off.

⛅Weather

Current location or a chosen city, °C or °F, and a forecast as wide as you like.

🔋Battery

Rings for your Mac, AirPods, keyboards, mice and trackpads.

🗒Sticky Notes

Your Stickies notes, shown directly in their colours.

⏱Focus Timer

A Pomodoro timer with automatic breaks and a notification at the end of each session.

💧Hydration

A countdown to your next glass of water, with a daily goal.

⚡Shortcuts

Run your macOS Shortcuts with one click.

📈AI Limits & Activity

Remaining allowance and reset times for Claude, Codex and Copilot, plus token history from local Claude Code and Codex logs.

Build & install

Requires macOS 15 or later and the Swift toolchain. The Xcode Command Line Tools are enough.

Clone the repository

git clone https://github.com/tk120404/dockerset.git
cd dockerset

Build and launch

scripts/build-app.sh --run

The app is built to build/Dockerset.app. Copy it to /Applications to keep it.

Open Settings

Settings opens on first launch. Later, right-click the Dockerset Dock, open Dockerset again from Finder or Spotlight, use the menu bar icon, or run open dockerset://settings.

URL scheme

Use these from Shortcuts, scripts or Focus automations.

URLWhat it does
dockerset://layout/<name>Switch to a layout (case-insensitive; percent-encode spaces)
dockerset://nextCycle to the next layout
dockerset://mode/native|custom|bothChoose which Dock is shown
dockerset://settingsOpen Settings

Permissions & privacy

macOS asks for each permission the first time a feature needs it. Nothing leaves your Mac except weather requests to Open-Meteo and, if you enable Copilot limits, a call through your own GitHub CLI login.
